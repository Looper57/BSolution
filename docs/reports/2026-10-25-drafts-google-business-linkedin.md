## B. Článek na LinkedIn (en; publikuje vlastník ze svého profilu nebo ze stránky B Solution)

**In-house lawyers in pharma across Central Europe: what companies are looking for**

When a pharmaceutical company in Prague, Budapest, Warsaw, Basel or Istanbul needs a new Senior Legal Counsel or Legal Director, it rarely needs "just a good lawyer". It needs someone who can close commercial agreements quickly and, at the same time, keep the business inside strict rules on promotion, pricing and reimbursement, interactions with doctors and pharmacies, and personal data. In regional roles, the same lawyer often supports several countries with different regulators.

Over the years we have recruited in-house lawyers for pharmaceutical companies in the Czech Republic, Hungary, Poland, Switzerland and Turkey, for global pharmaceutical groups and for generics manufacturers. Three things come up in almost every search:

1. **Regulated work, not only contracts.** The strongest candidates can explain how they handled promotion rules, healthcare-professional interactions or a compliance investigation, not only which contracts they negotiated.
2. **One lawyer, several countries.** Regional and EMEA roles ask for the local language, English, and the ability to work with a regional legal team and local management at the same time.
3. **The best people are not looking.** Most of the lawyers who fit these roles are in stable positions. They respond to a direct, discreet approach with a role worth discussing, not to an advert.

Market reports point the same way. In 2024 Major, Lindsey & Africa described flat hiring in big pharma, where legal, privacy and compliance functions were being restructured, and steady demand in biotech, especially for lawyers with product-launch experience and for functional roles such as regulatory, IP and compliance.

If you are hiring a Senior Legal Counsel or Legal Director in pharma, or you are a pharma lawyer thinking about the next step, we are happy to talk in confidence.
