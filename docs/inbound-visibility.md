# Client acquisition: inbound discovery

The owner's instruction of12November2026 is explicit: B Solution earns employer enquiries by being found when clients seek help. Visibility work serves international law firms and companies hiring in-house lawyers; Czech acquisition is secondary.

## How work is prioritised

- Start with an employer's real hiring problem, observed search intent and the existing canonical page best suited to it.
- Make expertise, relevant evidence, scope and a voluntary enquiry path clear on the public page.
- Keep foreign-market, brand and employer-intent results separate from candidate and aggregated-job traffic.
- Measure relevant impressions, clicks, qualified incoming enquiries and eventual mandates; a ranking or page view alone is not a client.
- Preserve useful cross-border context and language-specific content without creating duplicate landing pages for synonyms.

This workstream does not build customer prospect lists or send cold emails, unsolicited LinkedIn pitches, automated lead messages or mass directory submissions. Existing drafts mentioning external profiles/references are recommendations for consistent discoverable identity, not authority to contact anyone.

A candidate search commissioned by an employer is the recruitment service itself and is separate from how B Solution acquires the employer. This instruction does not remove candidate-search descriptions or change candidate consent/CRM rules.

No unverified client names, placement results, local offices, guarantees or prices may be added to strengthen a page. Existing commercial wording is not revised by an SEO block. Public search samples do not establish exact Google ranks. Private Search Console data stays outside this public repository.
